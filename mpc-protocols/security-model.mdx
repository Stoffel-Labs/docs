Use this page when your application has concrete fault-tolerance, availability, or adversary requirements. Stoffel provides a field-arithmetic HoneyBadgerMPC backend and a Feldman-committed AVSS backend. They serve different workloads and expose different privacy boundaries.

## Parameters

`n` is the number of computing parties. `t` is the maximum number of corrupted computing parties. Input and output clients are not included in `n`.

Stoffel configuration requires:

```text
n >= 4t + 1
n >= 5
t > 0
```

A degree-`t` Shamir sharing can be interpolated from `t+1` correct shares. Robust reconstruction needs additional distinct, authenticated evaluations when some supplied shares may be wrong. The `t+1` interpolation count is therefore not a complete Byzantine-fault threshold.[9]

## Preprocessing and online execution

Preprocessing generates random shares, multiplication triples, random bits, and fixed-point masks before application values are computed. Online execution consumes that material for input, arithmetic, and output.

The distinction matters operationally. HoneyBadgerMPC deliberately combines optimistic preprocessing with a robust online phase. Preprocessing may stop when a required party is unavailable; an existing material reserve can keep online computation running only until that reserve is exhausted.[1]

## Asynchronous safety and liveness

Asynchronous safety means message delay alone cannot make honest parties accept incompatible or incorrect results or cross the stated privacy boundary. A safe protocol may still stop.

Asynchronous liveness means honest parties eventually complete despite up to `t` faulty parties, assuming eventual delivery between honest parties and fair scheduling. It does not cover a permanent partition, missing required client input, or an exhausted preprocessing reserve.

Randomized asynchronous agreement can have an expected-round bound without a fixed completion deadline. FLP explains why asynchronous consensus cannot promise deterministic termination in every execution with even one fault.[14]

## Privacy and deployment assumptions

- Party-to-party and client-to-party traffic needs authenticated identities and confidential channels. Reliable broadcast provides agreement, not encryption.
- Random generators must be cryptographically secure.
- Masks, random shares, and triples must be consumed once, including across cancellation, retries, crashes, and restored state.
- `t` is the total corrupted-party budget, not one allowance for Byzantine parties plus another for crashed parties.
- The backends do not add differential privacy. Authorized outputs and repeated queries can still disclose application information.
- Constant-time execution, side-channel resistance, forward secrecy, and formal universal composability are separate deployment requirements.

## AVSS commitment boundary

Feldman commitments include `C_0 = sG` for secret scalar `s`. Anyone who can enumerate plausible values can test guesses against that public point. AVSS is therefore appropriate when a public commitment to a high-entropy cryptographic scalar is intentional. It is not a generic hiding representation for salaries, ages, binary choices, or other small-domain application data.[10]

AVSS share generation also does not by itself provide a complete distributed key-generation or threshold-signature protocol. Those protocols need their own key setup, nonce, transcript, verification, recovery, and output rules.

## Which backend fits stricter requirements?

Choose HoneyBadgerMPC for private arithmetic over application values when you can provision preprocessing and operate within its static-corruption model.

Choose AVSS when your application deliberately needs public commitments or curve-compatible scalar shares, and its commitment leakage is acceptable. Do not rely on its generated triples for malicious-secure multiplication without an additional validated product-relation mechanism.

If your requirement includes adaptive corruptions, guaranteed completion in both phases under all allowed faults, or a formally verified malicious-security composition, neither backend alone establishes that requirement.
